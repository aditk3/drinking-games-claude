# Drinking Games for 4 People

A reference list of drinking games that work with exactly 4 players, grouped by what you need to play them. Every entry follows the same shape so it can be parsed or rendered:

- **Needs:** what equipment or devices are required
- **Vibe:** how intense it gets (Chill / Medium / Heavy)
- **Style:** the kind of game (Luck, Skill, Social, Memory, Dexterity, Video Game, Endurance)
- **How to play:** the short rules
- **Tip:** optional note for 4 players

---

## 1. No devices

Games that need at most a deck of cards, dice, or cups. Nothing with a screen.

### 1a. Standard deck of cards

#### Kings Cup (Ring of Fire)
- **Needs:** Deck of cards, one center cup
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Luck / Social
- **How to play:** Fan the deck face down around a center cup. Take turns drawing a card and following its rule: 2 = you (pick someone to drink), 3 = me (you drink), 4 = floor (last to touch the floor drinks), 5 = guys drink, 6 = chicks drink, 7 = heaven (last to raise a hand drinks), 8 = mate (pick a drinking buddy), 9 = rhyme (go around rhyming a word), 10 = categories, J = make a rule, Q = question master (anyone who answers your question drinks), K = pour some of your drink into the center cup. Whoever draws the fourth King drinks the center cup.
- **Tip:** The universal starter. Agree on your group's card meanings before you begin.

#### Ride the Bus
- **Needs:** Deck of cards
- **Vibe:** Heavy
- **Style:** Luck
- **How to play:** Round 1: each player is asked four questions on four dealt cards: red or black, higher or lower, in between or outside, and which suit. Wrong = drink, right = give drinks. Round 2: a pyramid of face-down cards is flipped row by row; if you hold a matching card you assign drinks equal to the row number. Round 3: the player with the most cards left rides the bus, flipping cards from a row and drinking on every face card, restarting on each miss.
- **Tip:** Brutal for whoever rides the bus. Cap the bus at 5 or 6 cards if you want to survive.

#### Pyramid
- **Needs:** Deck of cards
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Memory / Bluffing
- **How to play:** Deal 4 cards to each player; they look once and lay them face down. Build a face-down pyramid (5 cards in the bottom row up to 1 at the top). Flip the pyramid one card at a time. If you have a matching card in your hand (or claim to) you assign drinks equal to that row number. The target can call bullshit; if you were lying you drink double, if you were telling the truth they drink double.
- **Tip:** Great with 4 because everyone remembers everyone's tells.

#### Horserace
- **Needs:** Deck of cards
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Luck / Betting
- **How to play:** Pull the four Aces and line them up as horses. Lay 7 cards face down as the track. Each player bets a number of drinks on a suit. Flip cards from the deck; whichever suit comes up, that Ace advances one space. When a horse passes a track card, flip it; the matching suit's horse moves back one. First horse to the end wins. Losers drink their bet; the winner hands out theirs.
- **Tip:** Have one person narrate like a race commentator.

#### Blind Man's Bluff (Indian Poker)
- **Needs:** Deck of cards
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Betting / Social
- **How to play:** Each player holds one card against their forehead facing outward without looking at it. Everyone can see every card except their own. Bet drinks on whether you have the highest card. Lowest card drinks the total pot.
- **Tip:** Bluff about other people's cards to mess with them.

#### Across the Bridge
- **Needs:** Deck of cards
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Luck
- **How to play:** Lay 10 cards face down in a line. Players take turns flipping the next card. Number cards are safe. Face cards mean you drink and extend the bridge: Jack = drink 1 and add 1 card, Queen = 2, King = 3, Ace = 4. Reach the far side to win.
- **Tip:** Easy to teach, surprisingly long if the face cards keep coming.

#### Screw the Dealer
- **Needs:** Deck of cards
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Luck
- **How to play:** The dealer looks at the top card. The player to their left guesses its rank. If wrong, the dealer says higher or lower and the player guesses again. Miss both and drink the difference between your guess and the card. Guess right on the first try and the dealer drinks 5, second try the dealer drinks 2. Lay used cards face up by rank. If three players in a row guess wrong, the deck passes.
- **Tip:** The dealer role is the dangerous one.

#### Higher or Lower
- **Needs:** Deck of cards
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Luck
- **How to play:** Flip one card. Guess whether the next is higher or lower. Wrong = drink, right = pass the turn. Streaks of 3 or more let you hand out drinks.
- **Tip:** Good warm-up or filler game.

#### President (Asshole)
- **Needs:** Deck of cards
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Skill
- **How to play:** Deal the whole deck. Shed cards by beating the previous play with equal or higher rank; the first to empty their hand is President, last is Asshole. Each round the Asshole deals, hands their two best cards to the President, and fetches drinks. The President can order anyone to drink. Standard extra rules: skips, bombs, and "drink when you can't play".
- **Tip:** With 4 you get President, Vice President, Vice Asshole, and Asshole, so every seat has a rule.

#### Circle of Death
- **Needs:** Deck of cards
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Luck / Social
- **How to play:** A Kings Cup variant with a different rule sheet, usually: Ace = waterfall, 2 = you, 3 = me, 4 = floor, 5 = guys, 6 = chicks, 7 = heaven, 8 = mate, 9 = bust a rhyme, 10 = categories, J = never have I ever, Q = question master, K = make a rule.
- **Tip:** Pick either Kings Cup or Circle of Death, not both; they are too similar.

#### Drunk Driver (Fuck the Dealer)
- **Needs:** Deck of cards
- **Vibe:** Heavy
- **Style:** Luck
- **How to play:** The dealer lays 5 cards in a row face down and flips them one at a time for the "driver". Number cards are safe. Face cards mean the driver drinks (J=1, Q=2, K=3, A=4) and the dealer adds that many new cards to the row. The driver keeps going until the row is cleared without a face card or the deck runs out.
- **Tip:** Take turns driving so nobody gets wrecked by bad luck.

#### Give and Take
- **Needs:** Deck of cards
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Luck
- **How to play:** Deal 4 cards to each player. Lay two rows of 6 cards face down: the Give row and the Take row. Flip row cards alternately, left to right. If you hold a matching rank for a Give card, give out drinks equal to that card's position (1 to 6). For a Take card, you drink that many.
- **Tip:** Row position 6 is the big one; keep an eye on who's holding doubles.

#### Bullshit (Cheat) with drinks
- **Needs:** Deck of cards
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Bluffing
- **How to play:** Deal the whole deck. Play cards face down in rank order (Aces, then 2s, and so on), announcing what you're playing. Anyone can call bullshit. If the caller is right, the liar drinks and picks up the pile. If wrong, the caller drinks and picks up the pile. First to empty their hand wins; the rest finish a sip per card left.
- **Tip:** Four people means fewer cards per rank, so lying is more tempting.

#### Drunk Spoons
- **Needs:** Deck of cards, 3 spoons (or any objects)
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Dexterity
- **How to play:** Pass cards around trying to collect four of a kind. When someone gets it they grab a spoon; everyone else scrambles. Whoever is left without one drinks and takes a letter (S-P-O-O-N). Spell it and you finish your drink.
- **Tip:** Chaotic and short. Keep breakables away from the table.

#### Slapjack with drinks
- **Needs:** Deck of cards
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Dexterity
- **How to play:** Flip cards into a center pile. When a Jack appears, slap it. Slowest hand drinks. Slapping a non-Jack means you drink.
- **Tip:** Add doubles (two of the same rank in a row) as a second slap trigger.

#### Any normal card game with a loser-drinks rule
- **Needs:** Deck of cards
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Skill
- **How to play:** Golf, Rummy, Crazy Eights, Go Fish, Egyptian Ratscrew, Hearts, Spades, Durak, Big Two. House rule: loser of each hand drinks, each penalty card is a sip, and the overall loser finishes their drink.
- **Tip:** Hearts and Spades are ideal for exactly 4 players.

#### Drunk Poker
- **Needs:** Deck of cards
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Skill / Betting
- **How to play:** Texas Hold'em with sips instead of chips. Folding costs a sip. Losing a showdown means drinking the pot (the sum of all bets).
- **Tip:** Set a per-hand cap so pots don't become unreasonable.

### 1b. Dice

#### Three Man
- **Needs:** Two dice
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Luck
- **How to play:** Roll two dice in turn. The first person to roll a 3 becomes the Three Man and drinks whenever any 3 shows up. A total of 7 means the person to the left drinks, 11 means the right drinks, doubles let you hand out that many drinks, and a 1 and a 2 passes the Three Man title.
- **Tip:** Being Three Man for a long time is the real punishment.

#### Liar's Dice (Perudo)
- **Needs:** 5 dice per person, cups
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Bluffing
- **How to play:** Everyone rolls under a cup. Bid on how many of a face are on the table in total. Raise or call. The loser of a call loses a die and drinks. Lose all your dice and finish your drink.
- **Tip:** Treat 1s as wild for faster rounds.

#### Mexico (Mexicali)
- **Needs:** Two dice, a cup
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Luck
- **How to play:** Each player rolls two dice. Read the higher die first (6 and 4 = 64). Doubles beat regular rolls; 2-1 "Mexico" is highest and doubles the stakes. Lowest roll of the round drinks.
- **Tip:** Add a "roll under the cup and bluff" rule for more tension.

#### Beer Die
- **Needs:** One die, a long table, cups
- **Vibe:** Heavy
- **Style:** Dexterity
- **How to play:** 2v2 at opposite ends of a table. Toss the die so it bounces once on the table and lands off the far end past your opponents' cups. Opponents try to catch it. Points for uncaught lands; drinks for sinking into a cup or dropping a catch.
- **Tip:** Needs space. Great outdoor game.

#### Ship, Captain, Crew
- **Needs:** Five dice
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Luck
- **How to play:** Three rolls to get a 6 (ship), 5 (captain), and 4 (crew) in that order. The remaining two dice are your cargo score. Lowest cargo drinks.
- **Tip:** Fast rounds; play best-of-5.

#### Drunken Yahtzee
- **Needs:** Five dice, scoresheet
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Luck / Skill
- **How to play:** Normal Yahtzee. Every zero on your sheet is a drink. A Yahtzee lets you hand out 5.
- **Tip:** Slow burn, good background game.

### 1c. Cups and table games

#### Beer Pong (2v2)
- **Needs:** 20 cups, ping pong balls, a table
- **Vibe:** Heavy
- **Style:** Dexterity
- **How to play:** Ten cups in a triangle each side. Throw balls into the opponents' cups; they drink any cup you hit. Sink all ten to win; losers finish whatever is left.
- **Tip:** Two re-racks per team keeps it fair.

#### Flip Cup (2v2)
- **Needs:** Cups, a table
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Dexterity / Endurance
- **How to play:** Teams line up on opposite sides. In relay, drink your cup then flip it upside down from the table edge using one finger. The next teammate goes only once the flip lands. First team to finish wins.
- **Tip:** Best of 5 so a single bad flip doesn't decide it.

#### Stack Cup (Rage Cup)
- **Needs:** Cups, 2 ping pong balls, a center cup
- **Vibe:** Heavy
- **Style:** Dexterity
- **How to play:** Two players start bouncing a ball into their own cup. When you make it, pass the cup left. If you catch the person next to you (the stack reaches them while they're still trying), they drink the center cup and refill it. First bounce in wins instantly.
- **Tip:** Works fine with 4; the stack comes around fast.

#### Quarters
- **Needs:** A quarter, a glass
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Dexterity
- **How to play:** Bounce a quarter off the table into a glass. Make it and pick someone to drink; keep going until you miss.
- **Tip:** Agree on a bounce rule to prevent lobs.

#### Civil War
- **Needs:** Cups, balls, a table
- **Vibe:** Heavy
- **Style:** Dexterity
- **How to play:** 2v2 beer pong but everyone throws at once, no turns. Each player defends 3 cups. When your cups are gone you're out. Last team standing wins.
- **Tip:** Loud and fast. Not a sipping game.

#### Slap Cup
- **Needs:** Cups, ping pong balls
- **Vibe:** Heavy
- **Style:** Dexterity
- **How to play:** Like Stack Cup but when you catch the next person you slap their cup away and they drink a cup from the center pile.
- **Tip:** Interchangeable with Stack Cup; pick one.

#### Boat Race
- **Needs:** Cups
- **Vibe:** Heavy
- **Style:** Endurance
- **How to play:** 2v2 relay chug. Each person drinks their cup fully and turns it upside down on their head before the next teammate starts.
- **Tip:** Two minutes of chaos, then move on.

#### Drunk Jenga
- **Needs:** Jenga set, a marker
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Dexterity / Social
- **How to play:** Write a rule on each block (drink 2, give 3, truth, dare, make a rule, waterfall, etc.). Pull a block, do the rule. Knock it down and finish your drink.
- **Tip:** Leave a few blocks blank as "safe" pulls.

#### Drunk Uno
- **Needs:** Uno deck
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Luck / Skill
- **How to play:** Draw 2 = drink 2, Draw 4 = drink 4, Reverse = everyone drinks, Skip = give a drink, Wild = make a rule, forgetting to say Uno = finish your drink.
- **Tip:** Works especially well with 4.

### 1d. Nothing needed

#### Never Have I Ever
- **Needs:** Nothing
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Social
- **How to play:** Say something you've never done. Anyone who has done it drinks. Use the ten-fingers version so the game ends when someone's fingers are down.
- **Tip:** Set a "no work stories" rule if things get too personal.

#### Most Likely To
- **Needs:** Nothing
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Social
- **How to play:** Read a prompt ("most likely to get kicked out of a bar"). On three, everyone points at someone. The person with the most fingers drinks that many sips.
- **Tip:** Have everyone pre-write prompts on scraps of paper.

#### 21 (Twenty-One)
- **Needs:** Nothing
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Memory
- **How to play:** Count around the circle from 1. Saying one number passes clockwise, two numbers reverses direction, three numbers skips a player. Whoever says 21 drinks and replaces a number with a word or rule for the next round.
- **Tip:** After five rules it's nearly impossible, which is the point.

#### 7-14-21 (Fizz Buzz)
- **Needs:** Nothing
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Memory
- **How to play:** Count up around the circle. Multiples of 7 and numbers containing 7 are replaced with a word or reverse the direction. Mistakes and hesitations drink.
- **Tip:** Add a second trigger number for round two.

#### Fuzzy Duck
- **Needs:** Nothing
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Memory / Speech
- **How to play:** Say "fuzzy duck" around the circle. Anyone can say "does he?" which reverses direction and changes the phrase to "ducky fuzz". Say it wrong (or spoonerize it) and you drink.
- **Tip:** Gets harder with every drink.

#### Thumper (Thumb Master)
- **Needs:** Nothing
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Attention
- **How to play:** One person is the Thumb Master. Whenever they put their thumb on the table, everyone must follow. Last to do it drinks and becomes the new Thumb Master.
- **Tip:** Runs in the background of any other game.

#### Medusa
- **Needs:** Nothing
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Luck
- **How to play:** Heads down. On three, everyone looks up at another player. If you lock eyes with someone, both drink.
- **Tip:** With 4 it happens constantly.

#### Would You Rather
- **Needs:** Nothing
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Social
- **How to play:** Pose a dilemma; everyone picks a side. The minority drinks.
- **Tip:** Ties mean everyone drinks.

#### Truth or Drink
- **Needs:** Nothing
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Social
- **How to play:** Ask someone a question. They answer honestly or drink.
- **Tip:** Use a question app or site if you run out.

#### Categories
- **Needs:** Nothing
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Memory
- **How to play:** Name a category (cereal brands, NBA teams, Marvel movies). Go around naming items. Repeat, hesitate, or blank and you drink.
- **Tip:** Loser picks the next category.

#### Rhyme Time
- **Needs:** Nothing
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Speech
- **How to play:** Say a word; go around rhyming it. Fail or stall and you drink.
- **Tip:** Ban "orange".

#### Word Association
- **Needs:** Nothing
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Speech
- **How to play:** Say a word related to the previous one, fast. Pauses, repeats, or unrelated words drink.
- **Tip:** Add a 3-second timer.

#### Paranoia
- **Needs:** Nothing
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Social
- **How to play:** Whisper a question to the person next to you ("who here would survive longest in a zombie apocalypse"). They answer out loud with a name. The named person can drink to hear the question, or stay paranoid.
- **Tip:** The best questions are specific and a little mean.

#### I'm Going on a Picnic
- **Needs:** Nothing
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Memory
- **How to play:** Each player repeats the growing list of items and adds one. Forget an item, drink.
- **Tip:** Alphabetical order makes it easier; random order makes it harder.

#### One Word Story
- **Needs:** Nothing
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Social
- **How to play:** Build a story one word per person. Stalling, breaking grammar, or laughing drinks.
- **Tip:** Record it and read it back at the end.

#### Drink While You Think
- **Needs:** Nothing
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Memory
- **How to play:** Name a celebrity. The next player must name one whose first name starts with the last letter of the previous celebrity's last name. You drink the whole time you're thinking. Same-letter names (like Michael Moore) reverse direction.
- **Tip:** Movie or athlete only versions are fun.

#### Cheers to the Governor
- **Needs:** Nothing
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Memory
- **How to play:** Count from 1 to 21. On 21 everyone says "cheers to the governor" and drinks, and the person who hit 21 replaces a number with a rule. Mistakes drink and restart.
- **Tip:** Almost identical to 21; choose one.

#### Waterfall
- **Needs:** Nothing
- **Vibe:** Heavy
- **Style:** Endurance
- **How to play:** Everyone starts drinking at once. Nobody can stop until the person to their right stops. The starter stops whenever they want.
- **Tip:** Use it as a punishment or round-ender rather than a full game.

#### Buzz
- **Needs:** Nothing
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Memory
- **How to play:** Count aloud around the circle, replacing any multiple of a chosen number (say 5) with "buzz". Miss it and drink.
- **Tip:** Layer "fizz" on a second number for a harder version.

#### Power Hour / Century Club
- **Needs:** A timer or a playlist
- **Vibe:** Heavy
- **Style:** Endurance
- **How to play:** Take a shot of beer every minute for 60 minutes (Power Hour) or 100 (Century Club).
- **Tip:** A one-minute-per-song playlist does the timing for you.

#### Song drinking games (Roxanne, Thunderstruck, Sweet Caroline)
- **Needs:** A speaker (optional)
- **Vibe:** Heavy
- **Style:** Endurance
- **How to play:** Roxanne: split into two groups, one drinks on every "Roxanne", the other on every "red light". Thunderstruck: pass the drink on every "thunder"; whoever is holding it drinks until the next one. Sweet Caroline: drink on every "so good".
- **Tip:** Short, loud, and a good break between card games.

#### Wisest Wizard
- **Needs:** Tape, empty cans
- **Vibe:** Heavy
- **Style:** Endurance
- **How to play:** Tape each empty can to the last to build a staff. The longest staff is the Wisest Wizard and can make others drink.
- **Tip:** Set a height limit.

#### Pizza Box Game
- **Needs:** An empty pizza box, a marker, a coin
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Luck
- **How to play:** Each player draws a circle with their name. Flip the coin onto the box. Land on a name, that person drinks. Land on blank space, draw a new circle with a new rule.
- **Tip:** The box fills up over the night and gets wild.

## 2. One shared device

One phone or laptop in the middle of the table. Everyone sits around it, passes it, or watches it.

### 2a. Dedicated drinking game apps

#### Picolo
- **Needs:** One phone
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Social
- **How to play:** Enter everyone's name. The app reads out rules, dares, and challenges addressed to specific players. Just follow along.
- **Tip:** Free base mode is plenty; paid packs add spicier categories.

#### Drink Roulette
- **Needs:** One phone
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Luck / Social
- **How to play:** Pick from 22 modes including spin wheels, card challenges, and timed games.
- **Tip:** Free; good variety when the group gets bored of one format.

#### Do or Drink
- **Needs:** One phone or browser
- **Vibe:** Heavy
- **Style:** Social / Dares
- **How to play:** Draw a card, do the dare or drink. Themed decks.
- **Tip:** Also playable in a browser, no install.

#### King of Booze
- **Needs:** One phone
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Luck / Social
- **How to play:** Board-style drinking game for up to 14 players with custom challenges.
- **Tip:** Has a BAC tracker if you want to be slightly responsible.

#### Game of Shots MAX
- **Needs:** One phone
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Mixed
- **How to play:** Bundle of classic drinking games plus a dice board mode.
- **Tip:** Free; handy as an all-in-one.

#### TOZ
- **Needs:** One phone
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Mixed
- **How to play:** 13 party games with a large challenge library. Works offline.
- **Tip:** Free with a subscription for the full library.

#### iPuke
- **Needs:** One iPhone
- **Vibe:** Heavy
- **Style:** Dares
- **How to play:** Dare-or-drink with points and an actual win condition.
- **Tip:** iOS only.

#### Drinkie
- **Needs:** One phone
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Social
- **How to play:** Five modes, 800+ cards.
- **Tip:** Free base, subscription for premium.

#### Drinkopoly
- **Needs:** One phone
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Mixed
- **How to play:** Card challenges with mini-games mixed in.
- **Tip:** Free with in-app purchases.

#### Gentle Jack: Omens
- **Needs:** One phone or laptop browser
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Social
- **How to play:** Free browser card game for 3 to 10 players with persistent "charms" that stack rules across the night.
- **Tip:** Nothing to install.

#### Truth or Drink (browser)
- **Needs:** One phone or laptop browser
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Social
- **How to play:** Free question generator with four modes. Answer honestly or drink.
- **Tip:** Good source of questions for the no-device version too.

#### PsyCat Games (browser)
- **Needs:** One phone or laptop browser
- **Vibe:** Mixed
- **Style:** Mixed
- **How to play:** Free web versions of Kings Cup, Never Have I Ever, Most Likely To, Truth or Drink, and more.
- **Tip:** Best option when you don't have a deck and don't want to install anything.

### 2b. Non-drinking apps with a drinking rule

#### Heads Up!
- **Needs:** One phone
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Speech / Dexterity
- **How to play:** Hold the phone on your forehead; teammates describe the word. Each pass is a drink for the guesser; the describers drink for every word missed in the round.
- **Tip:** Paid app ($1.99), some packs cost extra.

#### Psych!
- **Needs:** One phone
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Bluffing
- **How to play:** Everyone writes a fake answer to a trivia question, then votes for the real one. Pick a fake, drink. Fool someone, give a drink.
- **Tip:** The "And the Truth Comes Out" category is built for friend groups.

#### Charades! / Reverse Charades
- **Needs:** One phone
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Dexterity
- **How to play:** 2v2 timed rounds. Losing team drinks per word short of the winners.
- **Tip:** Reverse Charades (three act, one guesses) is funnier.

#### Cards Against Humanity (web)
- **Needs:** One phone or laptop
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Social
- **How to play:** Pass the device; each player picks a card, the Czar picks a winner, losers drink.
- **Tip:** Sites like allbad.cards run it free.

#### Bomb Party (JKLM.fun)
- **Needs:** One phone or laptop
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Speech
- **How to play:** Say a word containing the given syllable before the bomb goes off. Explode and drink.
- **Tip:** Also works with one device per person.

#### Skribbl.io (passed around)
- **Needs:** One laptop
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Dexterity
- **How to play:** One person draws, others guess out loud. Last to guess or nobody guessing drinks.
- **Tip:** Better with one device each.

#### Trivia (Sporcle, Trivia Crack, quiz sites)
- **Needs:** One laptop
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Knowledge
- **How to play:** Run a Sporcle quiz like "name all 50 states" or "name every NBA team". Every miss, everyone drinks. Or play Trivia Crack and drink per wrong answer.
- **Tip:** Make a custom Sporcle quiz about your friend group.

#### Spin the Wheel (any wheel site)
- **Needs:** One phone or laptop
- **Vibe:** Mixed
- **Style:** Luck
- **How to play:** Load a wheel with rules and penalties. Spin on your turn.
- **Tip:** Great for "what do we play next".

#### YouTube challenge videos
- **Needs:** One laptop or TV
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Knowledge
- **How to play:** "Guess the song in 1 second", "impossible quiz", lyrics quizzes. Everyone shouts; wrong or last drinks.
- **Tip:** Endless supply.

#### GeoGuessr (free mode)
- **Needs:** One laptop
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Knowledge
- **How to play:** Pass the laptop; each person places a guess. Farthest from the real location drinks.
- **Tip:** Country-only mode speeds it up.

#### NYT Games (Wordle, Connections, Mini Crossword)
- **Needs:** One phone
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Knowledge
- **How to play:** Pass the phone around taking turns. Every wrong guess the group makes means everyone drinks.
- **Tip:** Good "winding down" game.

#### Akinator
- **Needs:** One phone
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Luck
- **How to play:** Think of a character. If Akinator guesses it, you drink. If it fails, everyone else drinks.
- **Tip:** Obscure characters are the strategy.

#### Chrome Dino / Flappy Bird
- **Needs:** One phone or laptop
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Dexterity
- **How to play:** Pass it around. Lowest score drinks.
- **Tip:** Harder than it sounds after round three.

### 2c. Video, music, and console

#### TV show / movie drinking games
- **Needs:** A TV or laptop
- **Vibe:** Mixed
- **Style:** Social
- **How to play:** Pick a show and a rule sheet. The Office: drink when Michael says "that's what she said". Friends: drink when Joey eats. The Bachelor: drink on "journey". TV Tropes has a DrinkingGame page for most shows.
- **Tip:** Choose an episode everyone has seen.

#### Power Hour playlist
- **Needs:** One laptop or speaker
- **Vibe:** Heavy
- **Style:** Endurance
- **How to play:** Play a YouTube Power Hour mix (60 one-minute clips). Shot of beer at every change.
- **Tip:** Same as the no-device Power Hour, just automated.

#### Music Roulette
- **Needs:** One speaker, a shared playlist
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Knowledge
- **How to play:** Shuffle a playlist. First to name artist and song is safe; the others drink.
- **Tip:** Build the playlist from everyone's top songs.

#### Mario Kart
- **Needs:** Console, 4 controllers
- **Vibe:** Heavy
- **Style:** Video Game
- **How to play:** Drink per shell hit, finish your drink for last place, and the classic rule: you must finish a beer before the race ends but cannot drink while moving.
- **Tip:** 150cc, items on.

#### Mario Party
- **Needs:** Console, 4 controllers
- **Vibe:** Heavy
- **Style:** Video Game
- **How to play:** Drink per lost mini-game, finish your drink for last place, drink when you lose a star.
- **Tip:** Ten turns is plenty.

#### Super Smash Bros
- **Needs:** Console, 4 controllers
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Video Game
- **How to play:** Drink per stock lost. Winner hands out drinks.
- **Tip:** Items on for chaos.

#### Overcooked
- **Needs:** Console or PC, 4 controllers
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Video Game
- **How to play:** Drink on every burned dish and every failed order. Everyone drinks if you fail the level.
- **Tip:** Cooperative, so nobody gets singled out.

#### Just Dance / Wii Sports
- **Needs:** Console
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Video Game
- **How to play:** Lowest score drinks.
- **Tip:** Good physical break.

## 3. One device per person

Everyone joins a room or server on their own phone or laptop.

### 3a. Phones as controllers, one screen

#### Jackbox Party Packs
- **Needs:** One laptop or TV running the game, phones to join at jackbox.tv
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Social
- **How to play:** Quiplash (lose a matchup, drink), Fibbage (fooled, drink), Drawful, Trivia Murder Party (die, finish your drink), Tee K.O., Blather Round, Survive the Internet, Guesspionage, Job Job, Dictionarium. Simplest rule: last place each round drinks.
- **Tip:** The best option for exactly 4 with phones. Party Pack 3 and 7 are strong picks.

#### AirConsole
- **Needs:** One browser as the console, phones as controllers via a code
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Video Game
- **How to play:** Free. Has a drinking games section: Racing Wars (drink for power-ups and crashes), Tower of Babel, ClusterPuck 99 (drink on goals), Castle Hustle, Quiz (drink on wrong answers), Pad Poker, Silly World Series, Balloon Party, Tiles of Doom, and more.
- **Tip:** Nothing to install on anyone's phone.

#### Kahoot! / Quizizz / Blooket
- **Needs:** One laptop host, phones to join
- **Vibe:** Chill
- **Style:** Knowledge
- **How to play:** Host a quiz; everyone joins with a code. Wrong answer or lowest score each round drinks.
- **Tip:** Make a custom quiz about your friend group beforehand.

#### Trinks
- **Needs:** One phone each
- **Vibe:** Medium
- **Style:** Social

## 4. Bonus: board games and purpose-made decks

#### Drunk board games
- **Needs:** The board game
- **Vibe:** Mixed
- **Style:** Skill
- **How to play:** Catan: drink when robbed. Monopoly: drink on rent paid, finish on jail. Scrabble: sip per point below the leader. Chess or Checkers: sip per piece lost. Twister: drink per fall.
- **Tip:** Catan and Monopoly run long; set a time cap.

#### Purpose-made drinking card decks
- **Needs:** Buy the deck
- **Vibe:** Heavy
- **Style:** Social
- **How to play:** Buzzed, Do or Drink, These Cards Will Get You Drunk, Drunk Stoned or Stupid, Kings of Booze, Sotally Tober, Drink-A-Palooza. All designed for 4 or more.
- **Tip:** Buzzed and These Cards Will Get You Drunk are the most popular at 4.
